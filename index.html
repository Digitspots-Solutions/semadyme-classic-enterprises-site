About Crystal Grace Pipeline Services Co

Crystal Grace Pipeline Services Co (also known as Crystal Grace Midstream Engineering Limited) is a leading oil and gas engineering firm based in Lagos, Nigeria. With expertise in LNG project management and midstream engineering, we provide comprehensive pipeline services that meet international safety and quality standards.

Our team of experienced engineers and project managers brings decades of combined expertise in designing, installing, maintaining, and managing pipeline infrastructure across Nigeria's oil and gas sector.

We are committed to delivering projects on time, within budget, and to the highest standards of safety and environmental responsibility. Our client-focused approach ensures that every project receives the dedicated attention and technical excellence it deserves.

  • Client Satisfaction - Delivering value beyond expectations

Comprehensive Pipeline & Midstream Solutions

LNG Project Management

End-to-end management of liquefied natural gas projects from feasibility studies through commissioning. Our experienced LNG project managers ensure seamless execution, regulatory compliance, and optimal performance.

Pipeline Design & Engineering

Complete pipeline engineering services including route selection, hydraulic analysis, material specification, and detailed design documentation that meets Nigerian and international standards.

Pipeline Installation & Construction

Expert installation services for onshore and offshore pipelines, from trenching and welding to coating and commissioning, with full compliance to DPR and industry regulations.

Pipeline Integrity Management

Comprehensive integrity assessment programs including inspection, corrosion monitoring, risk assessment, and maintenance planning to extend pipeline life and ensure safe operations.

Midstream Engineering Solutions

Complete midstream infrastructure development including gas processing facilities, compression stations, metering systems, and flow assurance engineering.

HSE Consulting & Compliance

Health, Safety, and Environmental consulting services ensuring your pipeline operations meet Nigerian Content Development requirements and international best practices.

Project Commissioning & Start-Up

Systematic commissioning procedures, performance testing, and operational readiness services to ensure safe and efficient pipeline system start-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What regions of Nigeria do you serve?
We provide pipeline and midstream engineering services across Nigeria, with particular expertise in Lagos, Rivers, Delta, Bayelsa, and Akwa Ibom states. We can mobilise teams nationwide for projects of any scale.
Are you compliant with Nigerian Content Development requirements?
Yes, Crystal Grace Pipeline Services Co is fully compliant with all Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board (NCDMB) requirements. We prioritise local content in our operations and maintain all necessary registrations and certifications.
What standards do your pipeline services meet?
Our engineering and construction services comply with international standards including API, ASME, ISO, and DPR regulations. We maintain strict quality control and HSE protocols on every project.
How long does a typical pipeline project take?
Project duration varies based on scope, terrain, pipeline diameter, and length. A typical small to medium onshore pipeline project ranges from 3-8 months. We provide detailed timelines during project planning.
Do you offer maintenance services for existing pipelines?
Yes, we provide comprehensive pipeline integrity management including inspection, maintenance, repair, and rehabilitation services for existing pipeline infrastructure.
What makes your LNG project management different?
Our LNG project managers bring hands-on operational experience from major Nigerian LNG facilities. We understand the unique challenges of LNG projects in Nigeria and provide practical, results-oriented management.
How do I request a project quote?
Contact us via phone, email, or WhatsApp with your project details. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your requirements and provide a detailed proposal within 5-7 business days.
Do you work with international oil companies?
Yes, we have experience working with both Nigerian independent operators and international oil companies. We understand the compliance, documentation, and quality standards required by IOCs.
